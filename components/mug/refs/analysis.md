# Mug — reference analysis (step A of the component workflow)

Reference: `ref_speckled_mug.png` — Etsy listing "Irregular Ceramic Coffee Mug – Speckled, Neutral Aesthetic".

## Certain (read directly from the photo)

| Feature | What the photo shows | What was built |
|---|---|---|
| Shape | Hand-thrown, softly squared body; flares from a narrower base to a wider rim; rim height and outline uneven | Lathe body with 2nd–5th order radial wobble growing toward the rim, wavy rim height, slight lean |
| Rim | Thick, rounded | 4.2 mm wall, fully rounded rim |
| Glaze | Warm white, satin-gloss, no visible pooling | Two-tone warm white, roughness about 0.3 with fine variation |
| Speckles | Black, mostly tiny, some small, few larger; evenly scattered, also inside | 3 speckle layers (about 0.3–0.6 mm, 0.5–1 mm, 0.8–1.4 mm radius), slightly raised roughness |
| Handle | Chunky round ring (donut), attached on the right at mid-height, no hole shape other than a circle | Torus (outer 4 cm, tube 1.3 cm, hole about 1.7 cm), 10 % irregular thickness, fused to the wall |
| Contents | Espresso with brown crema about 1 cm below the rim | Crema surface 13 mm below the rim with a slight meniscus |
| Coaster | Wooden slice, light wood, bark edge, wider than the mug base | 11.5 cm slice, 14 mm thick, irregular outline, bark band with a pale cambium line |

## Inferred (not measurable from a product photo)

- Absolute size: mug about 10 cm wide x 8.5 cm tall, handle 4 cm. (The room's current mug is 11.1 x 10.5 cm; the new
  one is intentionally closer to the proportions of the photo.)
- Wall thickness, mug base (hidden by the coaster), coaster underside, number of growth rings and the crack.
- Speckle density: matched visually to about 0.05 per mm² for the tiny layer.

## Not copied

- Background (stone slab, pinecones, bowl, books) — not part of the component; the room has its own desk.
